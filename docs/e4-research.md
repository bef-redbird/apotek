# Repeatable candidate assessments and bounded policy research

E4 reuses accepted E3 models, predictions and source evidence. It adds an immutable
assessment and later-outcome lifecycle, actual advance-report intake, a finite
routing/fallback comparison, one residual-interval refinement and transparent
inventory scenarios. The private protocol freezes populations, methods and limits
before new comparative results. Existing history remains exposed retrospective
research. No model is promoted and scheduling remains disabled and uninstalled.

Only origin-time prices, attributes and group context enter features. Current ABC
and separately identified next-period ABC remain distinct. Final historical next
ABC is a conditional proxy. Actual reports preserve their bytes, receipt evidence,
period binding, duplicates and revisions. Unpublished next AIP and later ABC,
membership, attributes and survival never enter predictions.

Assessments pin all candidates, exclusions, inputs, models, calibration, fitting
cutoffs, predictions and selection reasons. Later official outcomes are immutable
separate versions; retries reuse the same content identity. Actual creation time
and claimed historical decision cutoff are different facts. A prediction produced
after target publication cannot become prospective by changing its label.

The frozen comparisons combine origin-supported broader/equal/package direction
routing with E3's fall-aware score and smaller threshold queue. Routing receives
its own earlier-held-out calibration. A genuine current-only fallback is compared
with the advance model's missing-code state. Monetary point forecasts remain
equal-peer conditional medians; a single fixed residual stratification tests
coverage and width together. Monetary peer routing is not implied.

Inventory scenarios share demand and constraints across policies. Public supplier
terms motivate explicit hypothetical compensation and returns assumptions, not
pharmacy-specific or historical coverage. Stock, outstanding orders, receipts,
cash, credits, expiry and valuation are separate. No unsupported sales margin or
realised savings is reported. Group demand is shared under an explicit substitution
assumption; a C classification does not mean zero demand.

The existing research dashboard reads pinned saved artifacts only. It never
queries the operating database, fetches sources, loads models or resolves outcomes
on page load. E1–E3 remain available through their existing arguments. Exact local
operating commands, results, source pins, resource use and acceptance are retained
in the private operating entry and E4 result area. [Progress](progress.md) records
what actually completed and what requires future observations.

The executed E4 result retains the original amount point forecast and rejects the
tested residual refinement: wider intervals did not improve overall coverage. The
observation candidate uses origin-supported direction routing, a true current-only
fallback and the fixed smaller threshold queue. Its lower fall exposure involves
an observed-rise trade-off at matched workload. Neither route is an automatically
promoted operational model. One actual report supports snapshot-specific reliability
only. The future current-only assessment is prospective-pending, not confirmation.

The command entry is `python -m bef_redbird.research.e4 --help`; the saved reader is
`streamlit run research_dashboard.py -- --e4 PATH-TO-INDEX`. Context JSON and pinned
artifacts are required for explicit preparation/outcome commands. No action is
scheduled or invoked on viewer load. Existing research recovery primitives restore
the E4 file capsule with shared immutable dependencies and no operating DB copy.
