comparison, historical period switching, ranked candidate detail, supported peer

The first actual historical Cito job took 6.779 seconds, including validation,
current-only and hybrid inference and publication, with a 392,458,240-byte peak
working set. Next-period current-only processing took 2.443 seconds. The corrected
nine-page PDF export took 1.230 seconds. These are local measurements, not promises
for other reports or machines. Operator intake and preparation have separate clocks.

The public delivery is deliberately limited to the application, the import and
source-pin closure of its retained inference/causal-input components, and relevant
synthetic tests. Older experiment matrices, dashboards, operator maintenance,
historical test suites, private validation receipts and recovery artifacts are not
part of this application PR. They remain preserved in the local operator workspace.
Required retained source components are copied unchanged rather than rewriting
verified inference to reduce file counts.

Independent private checks reproduced ranking and original-source outcomes; actual

reconnection and PDF download. Focused synthetic lifecycle, scientific-boundary and
