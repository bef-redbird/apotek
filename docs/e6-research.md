# Bounded selection, report-error and training-history research

E6 reuses accepted E2–E5 evidence and frozen feature contracts. The private
protocol fixes a finite matrix before comparative results. Existing history is
exposed retrospective research, including the additional earlier-year diagnostic
using later-selected settings. No operational model or purchase policy is promoted.

The four comparisons are current-only probability versus monetary ranking; actual
Cito codes versus wrong-code-only correction and no report; four exact screened
Huber outer fits; and three/six/full-history broad direction plus a fixed amount
objective. Conditional advance codes are separately named; missing codes retain
origin candidates and genuine current-only fallback. Earlier sidecar gaps remain
missing rather than filtering old training rows. Every fitted component and
calibration obeys its historical cutoff. Feature lookback is unchanged.

Natural queues retain their thresholds. Matched workload expansion is diagnostic,
including naturally rejected candidates, and never an order recommendation.
Unknown selected outcomes remain without replacement. Integer capital allocations
retain package/group caps and unallocated cash. Their quantities and price
movement do not establish demand, feasible purchasing or pharmacy profit.

The actual-report comparison retains the original E4 models and earlier-mature
calibration. A retrospectively corrected code is not evidence that its final value
was available early. Same-rule matched comparisons separate code effects from
queue-rule differences. One report cannot establish future report reliability.

The Huber loss remains a robust location estimate. Raw and nonnegative next-price
forecasts are both retained. Accepted uncertainty intervals are unchanged and are
not silently recentered on Huber. The history comparison uses one common fixed
recipe and separate raw-component/routed scores. Full-cohort fits, failed attempts,
scalar calibration and exact compatible reuse have separate receipts.

The existing dashboard accepts `--e6 PATH-TO-INDEX` and reads saved artifacts only.
It never fits, loads models, acquires files, queries the operating database, orders
or promotes. Earlier E4/E5 viewing remains available. The private operating entry
and E6 report hold exact launch, evaluation, replay and recovery commands and actual
verification status. The editable pharmacist meeting checklist remains separate.

## Measured conclusions

Probability selection remains the supported current-only reference. Monetary and
downside-aware rankings fail the same-workload replacement rule; a narrower natural
downside queue has some favourable measures but does not establish a replacement.
Capital exposure depends strongly on large quantities of cheap packs.

Correcting the actual report's 27 established comparable errors removes four natural
queue cases and changes aggregate one-pack movement by only DKK -18.45, with unchanged
downside. The actual report improves the same-workload comparison with no report in
this snapshot. Individual errors matter; one snapshot does not establish general
reliability or future financial value.

Huber improves conditional absolute error by 2.03% and root mean squared error by
4.72% versus the median-oriented reference, but current-only absolute error worsens
2.05%. It does not pass the frozen both-mode rule; existing references remain.

All 12 unweighted full-history fits succeeded on 3.60–3.97 million observations
from 2008. Three years remains the direction reference under the tested recipe.
Full history improves 2026 amount absolute error by 6.27% current-only and 9.48%
conditionally, but the 2025 diagnostic worsens absolute bias in both modes and
slightly worsens current-only root mean squared error. Six years and full history
qualify for further amount research, without operational promotion.

The two recency-weighted fits also completed. Weighting improves recent direction
errors versus unweighted full history, but gains versus three years are small and
calibration worsens. Only two summer tariff periods are scored. This does not
establish a broadly superior decay rule; amount weighting was not tested.

## Saved viewing and recovery

From the application repository, use an unused port:

```powershell
.venv/Scripts/python.exe -B -m streamlit run research_dashboard.py --server.address 127.0.0.1 --server.port 8517 --server.headless true --browser.gatherUsageStats false -- --e6 ../data/research-e6/index.json
```

`../data/research-e6/current.json` identifies the single saved index and acceptance
entry. The report retains per-fold, subgroup, coverage, allocation, uncertainty,
resource and failure details. The one capsule is `../data/recovery/research-e6-v1`;
`e6_delivery restore`, `verify` and `replay` are separate commands. Replay imports
captured source, requires the original E6 root to be unavailable, and blocks
fitting, network and operating-database access. Exact commands and byte/tolerance
results belong in the private operating entry and acceptance, including retained
shared dependencies and the final delivery-document supplement.
